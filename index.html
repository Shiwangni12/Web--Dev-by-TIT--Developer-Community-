cricket

Cricket is a popular outdoor game played with a bat and ball. It is played by two teams of 11 players on a pitch with two sets of three stumps called wickets. The goal of the game is to score as many runs as possible. Cricket originated in England and was introduced to India by the British in the early 1700s. Cricket is played at both national and international levels. There are many formats of cricket, each with different rules and duration. Some formats of cricket include Test matches, One-day internationals, and National League Systems. Cricket is played on a well-maintained pitch. People love cricket so much that small teams and groups play it on the roads and wherever there is enough space. Cricket is a popular sport in India and England.
